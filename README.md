# ❤️ Zajedno / Zusammen — Radoica & Malak

Dedicated, warm, and romantic web/mobile application designed specifically for **Radoica** and **Malak** to nurture their long-distance relationship, learn each other's native languages (Serbian & German), and plan their shared future.

---

## 🌟 Key Modules & Features

### 1. 👥 Dedicated Profiles & Creator Tracking
- Two predefined profiles: **Radoica** (Serbian native learning German) and **Malak** (German native learning Serbian).
- One-click profile switcher in the header.
- Every action (adding vocabulary, creating a to-do item, adding a book to the book club) records **who created it** with avatar badges.
- **Persistent Language Switcher (`SR 🇷🇸 | DE 🇩🇪`):** Toggles the entire app interface between Serbian and German instantly.

### 2. ⏳ Countdown Timer to Next Reunion
- Prominent hero widget on the Home Dashboard showing live countdown in **Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds**.
- Customizable trip title, location, and date (e.g., *"Božićno viđanje u Beču 🇦🇹"*).

### 3. 🎓 Dual-Language Learning Hub (German & Serbian) with Audio
- **Custom Vocabulary & 3D Photo Flashcards:**
  - Categorized: *Romance, Everyday, Travel, Food, Slang, Grammar*.
  - Realistic 3D flip animation (`perspective: 1200px`) using couple photos as backgrounds.
- **Audio Pronunciation (Dual-Language):**
  - Web Speech API speech synthesis on every card and dictionary row (`de-DE` for German and `sr-RS` for Serbian).
  - "0.7x Slow Speed / 1.0x Normal" toggle for difficult words.
- **Voice Notes Recording:**
  - Record your own voice notes to share authentic native pronunciation with your partner!
- **Daily Practice / Speed Quiz:**
  - Instant scoring, confetti rewards, and tracking of words mastered per user.

### 4. ✅ Shared To-Do List & Bucket List
- **Daily / Short-Term To-Dos:** Tasks to complete before trips (packing, buying tickets, reservations).
- **Couple Bucket List:** Long-term romantic dreams, restaurants, and destinations to experience together.
- Check off items, assign to Radoica, Malak, or Both, with due dates.

### 5. 📚 Shared Reading List (Book Club)
- Virtual couple bookshelf.
- Track books: *Want to Read*, *Currently Reading*, *Finished*.
- Add favorite quotes, thoughts, and star ratings (⭐).

### 6. 📸 Memories & Photo Album (47 Photos)
- All 47 photos from your shared album incorporated into a responsive lightbox gallery and card backgrounds.
